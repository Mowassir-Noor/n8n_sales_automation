# Nil's rules of 23 Nov 2026 (applied 25 Nov 2026)

This page describes what changed in WF0 to WF5, the sheet and the dashboard when Nil's decision log of 23 Nov 2026 was applied. Where this page and `workflows.md` or `google-sheet.md` differ, this page is the newer one.

## Sheet

**Leads**, new columns after `HubSpot Task ID` (AI to BD): `Hot Handled`, `Parent Lead ID`, `Wave`, `Product Type`, `Employees`, `Export Volume`, `Criteria Fit`, `Bespoke`, `Contact Role`, `Contact Source`, `General Email`, `Contact 2 Name`, `Contact 2 Title`, `Contact 2 Email`, `Contact 2 LinkedIn`, `Contact 2 First Email At`, `Follow-up 1 At`, `Follow-up 2 At`, `Follow-up 3 At`, `Reply At`, `ClickUp Stage`, `Approved By`.

**Settings**, new columns (Y to AD): `Calendar Link`, `Value Prop Exporter TR`, `Value Prop Exporter EN`, `Value Prop Brand TR`, `Value Prop Brand EN`, `WF3 Lock`.

**Actions**, new tab, append-only history: Timestamp, Lead ID, Company, Action, Person, Email, Subject, Summary, Approved By, Result.

`Segment` now holds Nil's segment names (`Hazır giyim / konfeksiyon`, `Entegre tesis`, `Kumaş / iplik / boyahane`, `Ev tekstili`, `İş kıyafeti`, `Sourcing ofisi`, `Marka`, `Hariç`); the old product label moved to `Product Type`. `Wave` is `1`, `2`, `Ayrı liste` or `Hariç`.

## Stages

`Stage` keeps its English values, because every workflow and the dashboard branch on them. Nil's ClickUp stage names are written to `ClickUp Stage` on every event. New `Stage` values:

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Sending` | WF3 has claimed the row and is about to send it. A row left here after a run needs a look. |
| `Bounced` | The mail did not arrive (ClickUp `Ulaşmadı`). Address is in Suppression, follow-ups stopped. |
| `Parked - Wave 2` | Fabric, yarn, dye house, home textile, workwear. Recorded, not mailed. |
| `Parked - Sourcing office` | Sourcing office of an EU brand. Recorded, not mailed. |
| `Parked - Bespoke` | Over 2,500 employees. To be written by hand. |
| `Not eligible` | Under 100 employees and under about 5 million USD export (both known), or out of scope. |
| `Waiting - 2nd contact` | The second contact's own row, before its turn. |
| `Closed - Other contact replied` | The other person at the firm replied, so this row stopped. |

## Email rules

- One offer: a 30-minute live demo. One link in the body: Settings `Calendar Link`.
- Language follows the company's country: Turkey gets Turkish, every other country English.
- The email is put together by code (WF2 `Assemble Email`, WF5 `Assemble Follow-up`). The model writes only the personal opening sentence and, for exporters, a short supporting paragraph and the subject. The value sentence comes from Settings (`Value Prop Exporter TR/EN`); brands get Nil's full template.
- First follow-up: Nil's four templates (exporter or brand, Turkish or English), no model. Second and third follow-up: the model writes the middle paragraph inside the same rules.
- `Check Draft` (WF2) and `Check Follow-up` (WF5) reject: any link other than the calendar link and texprove.com, "15 minutes", any offer other than the demo, price, package, catalog or sample wording, Tier claims, percentages, "fewer audits", competitor figures, any regulatory date other than 14 December 2027, 2027 (expected) and April 2028, and the wrong language. They require the 30-minute demo, the calendar link and the value sentence. The dashboard runs the same code (`dashboard/shared/email`).

## Targeting

- **WF0** searches garment makers and integrated groups first. Wave 2 companies it meets are added as `Parked - Wave 2`. Traders, wholesalers and domestic-only firms are not added.
- **WF1** classifies the segment, records employees and export volume, sets `Criteria Fit`, and looks for two people: the decision maker and the data owner (purchasing is never a target). The first mail goes to a published personal address, or else to the general address. The second contact gets its own row (`L123-B`, `Parent Lead ID` = `L123`) only when a second address exists. It also researches older `Researched` rows again (those with an empty `Contact Source`), keeping their contact and hook.
- **WF2** drafts only rows that WF1 has classified: wave 1 or brand, not bespoke.

## Sequencing

- First mail to the first contact. WF2 drafts the second contact's mail two days later; WF3 sends it only after three days without a reply (or straight away if the first address bounced).
- Follow-ups one week after that person's previous mail, at most three, then `Closed - No reply` a week after the third. WF5 writes the draft one working day before it is due and sends on or after the due date.
- Any reply from either person stops the other (WF4).

## Fixes

- **WF3** takes a lock (Settings `WF3 Lock`, expires after three hours) so two runs cannot send at once. Before each send it reads the row again and skips it if `Sent At` is filled or the stage is no longer `Drafted`. The Log and `Approved By` name the person who approved in the dashboard.
- **WF4** recognises delivery-failure notices in code: stage `Bounced`, address to Suppression, Telegram note. "Not interested" and opt-out replies add the company's domain to Suppression.

## Dashboard

The Settings page shows the calendar link and the value sentences read-only; change them in the sheet. After merging, the server needs `git pull` and `docker compose build dashboard && docker compose up -d dashboard`, and the `DB - Read` and `DB - Lead action` workflows need the new code (`npm run sync:n8n`).

## Not covered

- Texts for wave 2 and sourcing offices, and Nil's own texts for the second and third follow-up.
- Who the second contact is at brands.
